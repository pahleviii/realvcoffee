## Metrics Calculation Guide

### Engagement Rate
```
Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Followers × 100
Example: (95 + 24 + 12) / 3000 × 100 = 3.7%
```

**Good Benchmarks:**
- 1-3% = Normal
- 3-5% = Good
- 5%+ = Excellent

### Reach vs Impressions
- **Reach:** Unique people who saw your post
- **Impressions:** Total times your post was seen (includes repeats)
- **Engagement Rate:** Based on reach (not impressions)

### Growth Rate
```
Monthly Growth Rate = (New Followers This Month / Total Followers) × 100
Example: (150 new / 500 total) × 100 = 30% growth
```

**Healthy Growth:** 5-15% per month

## Problem Solving Guide

### Problem: Low Engagement (< 2%)

**Diagnosis:**
- Posting at wrong time?
- Content not interesting?
- Not replying to comments?
- Hashtags not working?

**Solutions:**
1. Change posting time (try afternoon instead of morning)
2. Review top posts - what made them successful?
3. Increase engagement with other posts (comment, like, share)
4. Ask direct questions in captions
5. Use trending sounds/music (if applicable)
6. Post more frequently (try 4-5x per week)

---

### Problem: No DM Inquiries

**Diagnosis:**
- CTA not clear?
- Contact info not visible?
- Website link broken?
- Not active enough?

**Solutions:**
1. Add clear CTA in captions ("DM us to order!")
2. Make sure bio has WhatsApp/website link
3. Engage more frequently (increase post frequency)
4. Respond quickly to inquiries (set expectation)
5. Feature DMs in Stories ("Someone asked...")

---

### Problem: Followers Not Growing

**Diagnosis:**
- Not enough posts?
- Wrong content mix?
- Not using hashtags?
- Not engaging with community?

**Solutions:**
1. Post minimum 3x per week
2. Mix content: 40% lifestyle, 30% product, 30% engagement
3. Use 15-20 hashtags per post
4. Follow/engage with 10-15 accounts daily
5. Repost customer content (tag them)
6. Collaborate with local accounts

## ROI Tracking (Orders from Social Media)

### Setup Tracking
1. Create unique discount code per campaign
   - Example: "INSTA20" (20% off from Instagram)
   - Example: "FB15" (15% off from Facebook)

2. Track usage of discount codes

| Month | Code | Used | Sales | ROI |
|-------|------|------|-------|-----|
| Jan | INSTA20 | 24 | 624k | +200% |
| Jan | FB15 | 18 | 468k | +150% |

### Calculate Social Media ROI
```
ROI = (Sales from Social / Spending on Social) × 100

Example: 
Sales = 1,092,000
Spending = 0 (organic)
ROI = Infinite (because free!)
```
