## Email Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Keep it personal (use [Name])
- Keep it short (max 200 words)
- Clear CTA (what to do next)
- Mobile responsive
- Test before sending

❌ **DON'T:**
- Spam people
- Too many links
- Complex design
- Inconsistent tone
