**Comment:** "Ini rasa gimana sih?"

> "Halo! RealV Signature kami itu smooth, creamy, manis alami tanpa bikin candu. Kombinasi espresso, palm sugar, dan silky oat milk yang udah kami fine-tune berkali-kali. Pengen coba? Bisa pesan online di realvcoffee.com atau dateng langsung ke toko kita 😊"

**Comment:** "Buka jam berapa sih?"

> "Kami buka setiap hari jam 08.00–20.00! Lokasi di Karang Pilang, Surabaya Selatan. Bisa pesan online atau datang langsung. Kita tunggu kamu! ☕"

> "Makasih banyak! Senang banget dengarnya 😊 Kapan balik ke toko kita lagi? Nanti kami udah ada menu terbaru juga!"

## 🎨 Content Creation Tips

### Photo Tips
- ✅ Good lighting (natural light is best)
- ✅ Product + context (not just product alone)
- ✅ Consistent filters/editing style
- ✅ Include people (when possible)
- ✅ Show the "moment" not just the object

### Caption Tips
- ✅ Start with hook (first line grabs attention)
- ✅ Casual tone (like talking to friend)
- ✅ Include CTA (what do you want them to do?)
- ✅ Emoji usage (2-4 per caption)
- ✅ Line breaks for readability

### Video/Reel Tips
- ✅ First 3 seconds crucial (hook them!)
- ✅ 15-30 seconds ideal length
- ✅ Trending sounds/music (check what's popular)
- ✅ Text overlay for clarity
- ✅ Include CTA at end

## 🚀 How to Grow Followers

### Organic Growth Tactics
1. **Be Consistent:** Post 3-4x per week minimum
2. **Engage Others:** Like/comment on related pages daily
3. **Use Hashtags:** Mix popular + niche hashtags
4. **Repost UGC:** When customers post about you, repost (with credit)
5. **Location Tagging:** Always tag Karang Pilang/Surabaya
6. **Cross-promote:** Share Instagram to Stories/Facebook
7. **Collaborate:** Partner with local businesses/influencers
8. **Timing:** Post when your audience is most active

### Growth Targets
- **Month 1:** 50-100 followers
- **Month 2:** 100-200 followers
- **Month 3:** 200-400 followers
- **Month 6:** 500-1000 followers

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Inconsistent Posting**
- Skip random days, post 5x then nothing
- Solution: Use scheduling tool, batch content creation

❌ **No Engagement**
- Post and disappear (don't reply comments)
- Solution: Set daily reminder to check & reply

❌ **Boring Content**
- Only product photos, no lifestyle
- Solution: Mix different content types (30% product, 40% lifestyle, 30% engagement)

❌ **Too Sales-y**
- Every post is "buy now, limited offer!"
- Solution: 70% value content, 30% promotional

❌ **Ignoring Comments**
- Negative comments or complaints go unanswered
- Solution: Respond within 24h, always be helpful
