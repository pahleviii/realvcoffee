## 🎓 Content Best Practices

### Do ✅
- Post consistently (better 3x week consistent than 5x then nothing)
- Engage authentically (reply comments like friend, not robot)
- Show personality (use tone yang kasual)
- Tell stories (behind-the-scenes, customer stories, team intro)
- Visual consistent (use same filters, colors, style)
- Listen to feedback (what customers say, improve based on it)
- Celebrate success (thank customers, share milestones)

### Don't ❌
- Don't be too salesy (balance promo with value content)
- Don't spam (quality > quantity)
- Don't ignore comments (reply semuanya)
- Don't copy-paste generic content (be authentic)
- Don't inconsistent tone (keep kasual & friendly)
- Don't overpost (max 3-4x per day)
- Don't buy followers (organic growth, though slower, is real)

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Problem: Engagement rate low
**Solution:**
- Check posting time (try different times)
- Improve photo quality
- Add more call-to-action (ask questions, engage)
- Use trending sounds (Reels/TikTok)

### Problem: Tidak ada orders via website
**Solution:**
- Check website functionality (test order flow)
- Simplify checkout process
- Add trust signals (testimonials, reviews)
- Promote WhatsApp option tapi encourage website

### Problem: Not enough photos for posting
**Solution:**
- Do photo shoots 1-2x per month
- Batch shoot (2-3 hours, 20+ photos)
- Use user generated content (ask customers)
- Mix product + lifestyle + team photos

### Problem: Tough to keep up with posting
**Solution:**
- Schedule posts 1 week ahead
- Create content batches (5 captions at once)
- Use scheduling tools (Buffer, Later)
- Assign to team member if possible
